About us

Four machines for moving earth, sold straight

TerraHaul is a UK online retailer for groundworkers and muck-away crews. We sell four machines that earn their keep on small sites: a mini excavator, a 500 kg tracked dumper, a petrol wood chipper and a stump grinder.

We do not run a depot. Every machine is crated at our supplier's warehouse overseas and shipped straight to you, arriving on the UK mainland in 7–12 working days with delivery paid. What you pay at checkout is the full price — no charges when it arrives.

Every machine comes with a 30-day return, two years on parts and a year on the engine, and our phones are answered Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
